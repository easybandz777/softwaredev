## QUARTERLY SEQUENCING STRATEGY

**Q1 (Posts 1–10) — Foundation Cluster.** Publish the high-volume TOFU pillars first. These earn backlinks, generate the most search impressions, and create the hub pages every later post will internally link to. Pillars include: build-vs-buy, custom CRM cost, MITRE ATT&CK explainer, Next.js + Stripe, and the Atlanta software dev page. Each subsequent quarter feeds traffic and link equity back to these pillars.

**Q2 (Posts 11–20) — MOFU Conversion Layer.** With pillars indexed, publish the problem-aware comparison and "signs you need X" posts. Each MOFU post internally links up to its parent Q1 pillar (passes equity) and down to the Q3 BOFU post (preps conversion path). This is where the funnel actually gets built.

**Q3 (Posts 21–30) — BOFU Capture & Local Authority.** Publish the comparison/template/calculator posts that capture buyer-intent traffic, plus the local-Atlanta + vertical-specific BOFU pages that close warm leads. By Q3 the Q1 pillars are aged and ranking, so these BOFU posts inherit topical authority.

**Why this order matters:** Posting BOFU first wastes link equity (nothing to link from). Posting MOFU before TOFU strands the comparison content with no upstream traffic. Q1 pillars are the trunk; Q2 + Q3 are branches.
