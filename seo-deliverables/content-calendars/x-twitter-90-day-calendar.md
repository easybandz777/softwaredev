## Engagement playbook

**Reply strategy.** Reply to every comment within 4 hours during the day. Tag the person by name. If they ask a real question, answer it in public — that's the post that gets the impressions.

**Quote-tweets.** Quote-tweet relevant founder threads from people in your reach 2-3x per week with a contrarian or builder-grounded angle. Do not subtweet. Do not vague-post.

**Lists.** Build private X lists for: (a) Atlanta-metro founders, (b) cybersecurity practitioners, (c) Stripe/Indie SaaS builders, (d) contractor/trades operators. Check each list once daily for thread fodder.

**Crossposting.** Mirror every thread to LinkedIn within 24h with a rewritten lead line. Mirror polls to LinkedIn polls when topical.

**DM follow-up.** When a thread gets above 5K impressions or a poll gets above 300 votes, DM the top 3 engaged accounts with a low-pressure offer (free /lead-magnet PDF or a 15-min audit). Do not pitch on the first DM.

**Tracking.** Tag every link with UTM params: `?utm_source=x&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=2026-q2-cal&utm_content=tweet-NN`. Fire `social_click` GA4 event on landing.
