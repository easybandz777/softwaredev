## 1. Tier-1 Universal Citations (10)

These are the highest-authority, must-have listings every business needs. Most are free, fast, and high-DA.

- **URL:** https://business.google.com
- **Cost:** Free
- **DA:** 100
- **Required:** NAP + website + categories + hours + photos
- **Approval:** Postcard or video verification (1–14 days)

### 1.2 Bing Places for Business
- **URL:** https://www.bingplaces.com/ (now also at https://www.bing.com/forbusiness)
