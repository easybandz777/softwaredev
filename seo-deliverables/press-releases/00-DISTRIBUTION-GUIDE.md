**Owner:** Bill Beltz
**Purpose:** Operational playbook for distributing every QUANT LAB press release to maximum effect across wires, journalists, and direct social channels.

## 1. Distribution channels by tier

### Tier 1 — Paid newswires (broadest reach, paid)
| Channel | Reach | Approx cost per release | When to use |
|---|---|---|---|
| **PR Newswire (Cision)** | National + ~5,000 newsroom subscribers | $805 base (US-only, 400 words) + $300 per 100 extra words | Major milestones: funding, big launches, founder hires |
| **GlobeNewswire (Notified)** | National + indexed by Google News | $375 base US distribution | Mid-tier company news, product launches |
| **Business Wire** | National + Bloomberg/Reuters terminals | $760 base (US-only, 400 words) | Financial-adjacent news, partnerships |
| **Accesswire (Newsfile)** | National + financial sites | $429 base | Cost-effective national wire |

**Recommendation:** Use **GlobeNewswire** as the default paid wire for QUANT LAB releases — best price/Google-News ratio. Reserve **PR Newswire** for one truly major announcement per year (e.g., a Series A close or a 100-engagement milestone).

### Tier 2 — Free wires and aggregators (no cost, indexed)
| Channel | Reach | Cost | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **OpenPR** | Aggregator, ~50k UVs/mo | Free | Submit via web form; 24–48h moderation |
| **PR.com** | Aggregator, indexed by Google News | Free (basic) | Free tier accepts ~500-word releases |
| **PRLog** | Aggregator | Free (basic) | Lower-quality backlink but indexed |
| **EIN Presswire** | Mid-tier wire, journalists subscribe | $99–$399 (cheapest tier accepts free for some categories) | Has a "free for nonprofits/regional" loophole — worth applying |
| **IssueWire** | Aggregator | Free | Submit each release |
| **24-7 Press Release** | Aggregator | Free (basic) | Reasonable indexing |

**Recommendation:** Push **every** QUANT LAB release through **all** Tier 2 free wires — total effort is ~45 minutes per release, and the aggregate backlink + brand-mention value compounds. Use a consistent boilerplate to speed submission.

### Tier 3 — Direct-to-journalist outreach (highest-leverage, hand-curated)
Direct pitches to the 35 journalists from the wave-4 outreach package. Send each release to a curated subset of those 35 based on beat fit (regional Georgia tech, custom software, cybersecurity, fintech, founder profiles).

Pitch via personal email — never via the wire-bulk-send tool. Subject line is the release headline. Body is 3 sentences max + the release URL. No PDF attachments unless requested.

**Direct-pitch list segmentation (from the wave-4 35-outlet package):**
- **Regional Georgia outlets (10):** Macon Telegraph, Atlanta Business Chronicle, Atlanta Inno, Hypepotamus, SaportaReport, Atlanta Magazine business desk, AJC Business, Macon Magazine, GeorgiaTrend, Albany Herald
- **National tech outlets (10):** TechCrunch (regional desk), VentureBeat, GeekWire, CIO Dive, Tech.co, Built In, Future Founders, The Information (tip line), Crain's Tech, Tech in Atlanta
- **Industry trade pubs (8):** Custom Coders, Dark Reading (security beat), CSO Online, ReadWrite, AlleyWatch, FinTech Magazine, Healthcare IT News, RetailDive
- **Podcasts and newsletters (7):** TWiT, Founders Journey, Tech Disrupt, This Week in Startups, The Pragmatic Engineer, Bootstrapped Web, Indie Hackers Newsletter

### Tier 4 — Owned/social channels (free, immediate)
- **LinkedIn:** Personal post from Bill linking the release. Add 3–4 sentences of founder color commentary. Tag relevant clients with permission.
- **X (Twitter):** Thread (5–8 tweets) summarizing the release. Pin if it's a major announcement.
- **Newsletter:** Mention in the next issue with one paragraph + link. Do not dedicate a full issue to the release.
- **`/newsroom`:** Publish the release on the QUANT LAB newsroom (already automated).
- **Reddit r/Macon, r/Atlanta, r/Georgia:** Regional subreddits — submit only if the release is regionally interesting (founding announcement, expansion to a new GA city). Do **not** spam every release here.

## 2. Distribution sequence per release

A standard release follows this 5-day rollout.

### Day -2 (Sunday before release)
- Final copy review. Verify boilerplate, contact info, headline.
- Pull the embargo-cover list (if embargoed).
- Pre-draft LinkedIn + X copy.

### Day 0 (Tuesday — release day)
- **8:00 AM ET:** Submit to GlobeNewswire (paid). Tier 1 wire goes first for first-mover indexing.
- **9:30 AM ET:** Submit to all Tier 2 free wires in parallel. Use copy-paste with consistent boilerplate.
- **10:00 AM ET:** Send direct emails to top 10 Tier 3 journalists (segment by beat).
- **11:00 AM ET:** Publish on `/newsroom` (technically already live, but ensure release date matches).
- **11:30 AM ET:** Bill posts on LinkedIn + X.
- **2:00 PM ET:** Second batch of journalist emails (next 15).
- **4:00 PM ET:** Newsletter snippet drafted for the next issue.

### Day 1 (Wednesday)
- Reply to any journalist who responded same-day.
- Final batch of journalist emails to remaining 10.
- Check Google News indexation (search `"QUANT LAB USA"` + release date).

### Day 3 (Friday)
- Compile early coverage list (mentions, reprints, backlinks).
- Reply to any inbound from podcasts or speaker bookings.

### Day 7 (next Tuesday)
- Newsletter ships with one-paragraph mention.
- Compile final coverage report (see section 4).

## 3. Embargo strategy

Use embargoes sparingly. They work for major announcements where you want simultaneous coverage from multiple outlets, not for routine releases.

**When to embargo:**
- Funding announcements
- Major partnership reveals
- Big product launches with simultaneous PR push

**When NOT to embargo:**
- Cost guides, free tool releases, milestone counts — these don't benefit from coordinated coverage
- Anything time-sensitive that loses value if delayed

**Embargo mechanics:**
- Email Tier 3 journalists 3–5 business days before release date.
- Subject: `EMBARGOED until [Tuesday, Date] 9:00 AM ET — [Headline]`
- Include the full press release inline.
- One paragraph at top stating the embargo terms.
- Follow up with anyone who doesn't acknowledge within 24h.
- Do not push to wires or social until the embargo lifts.

## 4. Response monitoring

### Free tools
- **Google Alerts:** Set alerts for `"QUANT LAB USA"`, `"QUANT LAB USA INC"`, `"Bill Beltz" software`, `"QUANT LAB" Macon`. Email digest daily.
- **Google News tab:** Manual check 24h, 72h, and 7 days post-release.
- **X search:** Monitor `quantlabusa`, `Bill Beltz software`, `QUANT LAB Macon`.
- **LinkedIn search:** Monitor for re-shares and quote citations.
- **Talkwalker Alerts (free):** Backup to Google Alerts; sometimes catches mentions Google misses.

### Paid (when budget allows)
- **Meltwater** ($5–10K/yr): Full media monitoring, sentiment, top journalist relationship CRM
- **Cision Monitoring** ($4–8K/yr): Wire-native monitoring; tight integration with paid PR Newswire distribution
- **Mention.com** ($31/mo): Mid-tier; better than free, cheaper than Meltwater

**Recommendation for 2026:** Stay on free tools. Revisit Meltwater at 50-engagement milestone or first six-figure press hit.

### Coverage report template (per release)
After Day 7, compile a one-page coverage report:
- Total wire publications (count)
- Direct journalist coverage (list: outlet, journalist, URL, sentiment)
- Social engagement (LinkedIn impressions, X engagement)
- Backlink count (Ahrefs spot-check)
- Inbound leads with `newsroom-release-*` source attribution (count + names)
- Notes for next release

## 6. Direct-pitch email template

Use this skeleton for the 35 wave-4 journalists. Replace bracketed sections per release and per journalist.

```
Subject: [Release headline as subject — exact match]

Hi [First name],

Quick heads-up — we [one sentence describing what's new, e.g., "just shipped a free 2026 software-development cost guide with public ranges and four interactive calculators"]. Given your work on [reference to specific recent article they wrote — proves you read them], I thought it might be useful for [their beat / their audience].

The release is here: [URL to /newsroom/<slug>]

Happy to send the underlying numbers, an exclusive angle for [their outlet], or a 15-min call if it's useful. If not, no worries.

— Bill Beltz
Founder, QUANT LAB USA INC
(770) 652-1282 · beltz@quantlabusa.dev
```

**Rules for journalist outreach:**
- Always reference a specific recent piece they wrote (proves it's not a blast).
- Three sentences max. Journalists scan; long emails get deleted.
- One URL. Never more.
- Sign with full name + direct phone. Builds credibility.
- Follow up exactly once, 5 business days later. Then stop.

## 8. What success looks like — per release benchmarks

- **Tier 1 paid wire:** 75–200 syndicated publications (mostly auto-aggregators)
- **Tier 2 free wires:** 20–50 additional aggregator pickups
- **Tier 3 direct:** 2–5 journalists open the email; 1–3 reply; 0–1 publishes original coverage
- **Tier 4 owned:** 1,500–4,000 LinkedIn impressions; 500–1,200 X impressions; 50–100 newsroom page views
- **Lead attribution:** 1–4 inbound consults tagged `newsroom-release-*` in the 14 days post-release

**Stretch:** 1 major outlet pickup per year (Atlanta Business Chronicle, TechCrunch regional, etc.) — that requires either luck or a genuinely newsworthy story. The 5 releases in this initial batch are foundation-laying; the bigger pickups come from cumulative momentum and the next 5–10 releases.
