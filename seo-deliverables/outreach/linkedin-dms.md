# Sequence Tracking & Optimization

## Weekly cadence per ICP
- **Mon-Fri 9-10 AM ET:** Send 5 new connection requests per ICP (rotate ICPs day to day)
- **Mon-Fri 4-5 PM ET:** Send follow-ups to accepted connections
- **F1:** 1 business day after accept
- **F2:** 4 days after F1
- **F3:** 4 days after F2 (so Day 8 total from accept)

## Target metrics
- Connection accept rate: 32% target (floor 22%, kill ICP if below for 2 weeks)
- F1 reply rate on accepts: 14% target
- F2 reply rate on F1 non-responders: 7% target
- F3 reply rate on F2 non-responders: 4% target
- Qualified-reply rate full sequence: 12% target
- Booked-call rate from qualified replies: 40% target

## A/B variant discipline
- Run 50/50 split Variant A vs Variant B per ICP for first 100 requests
- After 100, kill the loser
- Do not run more than 2 variants concurrently

## Personalization floor
Every connection request must include at least one specific detail from the prospect's profile. If you cannot fill the bracket personalization variables honestly in under 90 seconds, skip the prospect.

## Stop conditions
- Prospect replies (any sentiment) - stop and log
- Prospect changes jobs - stop, restart in 6 weeks if new role still fits ICP
- Connection request declined - 90-day cooldown

## Escalation
If a prospect connects, engages with your posts, but does not reply to F1/F2/F3: find their work email (Hunter.io, Apollo, RocketReach) and send one 60-word email with subject "Following up - [Company]." Reference the LinkedIn thread. No more than one email. Move on after.
