# Template: Broken-Link Replacement Pitch

**Use case:** Notifying a page owner that one of their outbound links is broken and offering your resource as a replacement
**Best for:** Resource pages, "best X for Y" roundups, blog posts older than 18 months
**Hit rate:** 1 in 3-5 when execution is honest and the replacement genuinely fits
**Time investment per pitch:** 15-25 minutes (link verification + write)

---

## Subject Line A/B/C Variants

**Variant A (direct factual):**
> Broken link on your [Page Title]

**Variant B (friendly heads-up):**
> Heads up - broken link on [Page Title]

**Variant C (low-stakes):**
> Quick note: [Page Title] has a 404

**Avoid:**
- "Broken link found!" (spammy)
- "Help me fix your page" (transactional framing)
- "Link exchange opportunity" (Google guidelines violation - never use)

## Hard rules

- Never claim you "found their page on a Google search for [target keyword]" - that reads as SEO outreach
- Never offer money or services in exchange for a link insertion
- Never ask them to add the link without first acknowledging the broken link is the actual reason for the email
- Never send if your replacement resource does not actually cover what the original covered - they will check
- Never resend the same pitch with a different replacement resource - one shot per page

---

## Pre-send checklist

- [ ] httpstatus.io confirms the linked URL returns 404 (or moved-with-no-redirect)
- [ ] Wayback shows the original resource existed and aligns with what you offer as replacement
- [ ] Target page is DR 50+ (verified via Ahrefs, Moz, or Ubersuggest)
- [ ] Target page was updated in last 18 months (check footer date or Wayback)
- [ ] Reference a specific section of the target page (not generic compliment)
- [ ] Your replacement resource is genuinely substantive (2,000+ words, with assets)
- [ ] Logged in tracking-tracker.md

---

## Follow-up cadence

3-touch sequence:
- Send: Day 0
- Follow-up #1: Day +14, single-line "Bumping this in case it got lost"
- Stop after Day +14. Do NOT pursue beyond a single follow-up - this template is meant to be low-friction.
