# Template: Podcast Appearance Pitch

**Use case:** Pitching yourself as a guest on a startup / founder / dev / cybersecurity podcast
**Best for:** Indie Hackers Podcast, Bootstrapped Founder, How To SaaS, Software Engineering Daily, My First Million, Founder's Story, Below The Line, Darknet Diaries (specific cybersecurity)
**Hit rate:** 1 in 5-10 for cold pitches; 1 in 2-3 with substantive proof of community presence (e.g., IH posts, prior podcasts)
**Time investment per pitch:** 30-45 minutes (listen to recent episode + write)

---

## Subject Line A/B/C Variants

**Variant A (specific value):**
> [Specific story or expertise] for [Podcast Name]

**Variant B (anti-cliche):**
> [Counter-conventional positioning that flips a podcast trope]

**Variant C (geographic / vertical hook):**
> [Place + Industry] founder pitch for [Podcast Name]

**Examples for QL:**
- A: "Bootstrapping a 14-client dev shop from Macon"
- B: "Anti-Silicon-Valley founder, Macon GA"
- C: "Macon, GA dev shop owner - blue-collar SaaS niche"

## Hard rules

- Never pitch a podcast you have not listened to (the host will sniff it out instantly)
- Never pitch a topic the show covered in the last 60 days
- Never pitch "I have an interesting story" without naming the story in the email
- Never copy-paste pitches across multiple podcasts (each must be tailored)
- If pitching a podcast with paid sponsorship slots (e.g., MFM, Below the Line), do NOT confuse "guest pitch" with "sponsor inquiry"

---

## Pre-send checklist

- [ ] Listened to at least 1 episode from the last 30 days
- [ ] Referenced the episode specifically in the opening sentence
- [ ] Confirmed your topic has not been covered in last 60 days
- [ ] Built proof-of-presence (IH posts / prior podcasts) BEFORE pitching the bigger shows
- [ ] All [bracketed] placeholders replaced
- [ ] Subject line under 55 characters
- [ ] Send Tuesday-Thursday, 9-11 AM ET
- [ ] Logged in tracking-tracker.md

---

## Follow-up cadence

3-touch sequence:
- Send: Day 0
- Follow-up #1: Day +14 (single line: "Bumping this in case it got lost")
- Follow-up #2: Day +45 (final, asks for redirect to right producer if applicable)
- Stop after Day +45
