# Template: Expert Quote / HARO Response

**Use case:** Responding to a journalist query on HARO, Qwoted, Featured.com, SourceBottle, Help-A-B2B-Writer, Source-of-Sources, #JournoRequest
**Best for:** Daily query response workflow; ongoing PR pipeline
**Hit rate:** 15-30% response→publication when execution is fast and specific
**Time investment per response:** 10-20 minutes

## Hard rules

- Respond within 4 hours when possible; 24 hours absolute max
- Never respond with a generic answer - journalists need specifics
- Always include ONE quantified outcome or data point
- Always state your position clearly in the first sentence
- Always include the 50-word bio + URL backlinks at the end
- Never lie about credentials or invent numbers
- Never respond to a query you cannot speak to honestly
- Anonymize clients without compromising the credibility of the quote

---

## What journalists actually want (use this checklist):

- [ ] A clear, opinionated position (not "well, it depends")
- [ ] A specific client outcome or data point
- [ ] An actionable takeaway readers can use
- [ ] A short, quotable sentence they can lift directly
- [ ] Your verifiable credentials (URL + LinkedIn)

If your response does not have all 5, rewrite it.

---

## Quotable-sentence design

The journalist will lift ONE sentence from your response. Design that sentence deliberately:

**Bad:** "There are many factors to consider when choosing a Stripe integration approach."

**Good:** "Most Stripe integration failures we see in production are not API misuse - they are webhook handlers that assume retries will not happen, which they will, 18 hours later, on Christmas Eve."

Make your quotable sentence:
- Under 35 words
- Has a number, a specific noun, or a counter-intuitive turn
- Can stand alone outside the email
- Names a real problem they have heard about

## After publication

When a quote lands, log:
- Publication URL
- Publication DR
- Quote excerpt
- Backlink type (dofollow / nofollow)
- Whether linked to quantlabusa.dev
- Share the publication on LinkedIn, Twitter, and reply to the journalist with a thank-you

If the journalist did NOT link to quantlabusa.dev, reply within 48 hours of publication asking politely if they would consider adding the link (some publications have policies; others just forget).
