# Template: Guest Post Pitch

**Use case:** Pitching a guest article to a software dev / tech / cybersecurity publication
**Best for:** Smashing Magazine, CSS-Tricks, The New Stack, InfoQ, freeCodeCamp, CSO Online, Stack Overflow Blog, A List Apart
**Hit rate:** 1 in 5-8 for cold pitches; 1 in 2-3 with strong personalization
**Time investment per pitch:** 30-45 minutes (research + write)

---

## Subject Line A/B/C Variants (test all three across batches)

**Variant A (specific topic):**
> Pitch: [Specific article angle in 4-6 words]

**Variant B (provocative claim):**
> [Claim that flips a common assumption]

**Variant C (case-study hook):**
> Case study: [Specific outcome from your work]

**Examples for QL:**
- A: "Pitch: WCAG 2.2 Stripe checkout on slow mobile"
- B: "Why we put tenant isolation in Postgres, not middleware"
- C: "Case study: 11 techniques, standard user to Domain Admin"
