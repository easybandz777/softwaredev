# Template: Roundup Inclusion Pitch

**Use case:** Asking to be added to an existing "Best X in Y" roundup post
**Best for:** "Best software dev firms in Atlanta", "Best pentest companies", "Top Stripe consultants", "Best Macon SMB tech firms"
**Hit rate:** 1 in 4-5 for good fits
**Time investment per pitch:** 15-20 minutes (read the page + write)

---

## Subject Line A/B/C Variants

**Variant A (suggestion-framed):**
> Suggestion for your [Page Title] roundup

**Variant B (resource-framed):**
> Resource: Add QUANT LAB USA to your [Page Title]

**Variant C (gap-framed):**
> [Page Title] - one missing firm

**Avoid:**
- "Add my company to your list" (transactional)
- "Increase your page traffic" (false promise; spammy)
- "[Company] partnership opportunity" (vague)

## Per-target customization checklist

Before sending, look at the page and answer:

1. **What are their ranking criteria?**
   - (Years in business / revenue / employee count / portfolio / methodology / reviews / awards)
2. **Which of those do you meet honestly?**
3. **What format does each listed firm have?**
   - (Logo + blurb / detailed profile / table row / case-study quote)
4. **What is their update cadence?**
   - (Annual / monthly / static / wiki-style)
5. **Have they updated this page in the last 18 months?**
   - If not, deprioritize

Customize your 3 "reasons we fit" bullets to match the criteria you found.

---

## Hard rules

- Never offer money or services in exchange for inclusion (Google guidelines violation)
- Never use "follow me back" or "let's exchange links" language
- Never copy-paste the same pitch across 20 roundups - each must reference the specific page
- Never claim qualifiers you cannot honestly meet
- Never pitch a page that has been static for 2+ years - they will not update for you either

---

## Pre-send checklist

- [ ] Page is at least DR 50

- [ ] Referenced one specific firm or ranking criterion from the page
- [ ] All 3 "reasons we fit" bullets map to that page's actual criteria
- [ ] All [bracketed] placeholders replaced

## Follow-up cadence

3-touch sequence:
- Send: Day 0
- Follow-up #1: Day +14 (single line: "Bumping this in case it got lost")
- Follow-up #2: Day +30 (final: "If this is not a fit, no worries - happy to revisit when [you hit a higher qualifier]")
- Stop after Day +30
