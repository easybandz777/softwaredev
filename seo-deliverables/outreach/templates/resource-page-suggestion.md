# Template: Resource Page Suggestion

**Use case:** Suggesting your resource (guide, template, case study, OSS repo) be added to an existing resource page
**Best for:** University career-services pages, chamber resource pages, industry association resource libraries, "Tools we love" pages on consultancy / agency sites
**Hit rate:** 1 in 5-7 when the resource genuinely fits
**Time investment per pitch:** 15-30 minutes (find a fitting page + write)

---

## Subject Line A/B/C Variants

**Variant A (value-framed):**
> Suggestion for your [Resource Page Title]

**Variant B (gift-framed):**
> Free resource for your [Topic] page

**Variant C (specific-add):**
> Add [Your Resource] to your [Page Title]?

**Avoid:**
- "Quick link request" (transactional, low-effort)
- "Link insertion opportunity" (industry jargon that triggers spam filters)
- "Mutual benefit" (red flag for shady SEO)

## Best target categories for QL resource suggestions

- **University career services / CS department pages** - "Resources for our CS students" pages often link OSS repos, technical guides; high-DR .edu links
- **Local chamber resource pages** - "Small business technology resources" pages on chamber sites; relevant for Macon and Atlanta-area chambers
- **Industry association libraries** - TAG, OWASP Atlanta, ISC2 Atlanta chapter resource pages
- **Consultancy / agency "Tools we recommend" pages** - non-competitive consultancies in adjacent verticals (e.g., a marketing agency's "Tools for SaaS founders" page)

---

## Hard rules

- Resource must actually exist and be substantive
- Never claim a resource is "free" if it requires email opt-in unless you say "free with email signup"
- Never pitch a resource you would not be proud to send a friend
- Never blanket-pitch the same resource to 50 pages - each pitch must reference the page's specifics

---

## Pre-send checklist

- [ ] Resource actually exists at the URL you are linking
- [ ] Resource is at least 2,000 words OR is a substantive downloadable/tool
- [ ] Page DR is 40+ (lower bar than other pitches because resource pages often hide higher value)
- [ ] Referenced one specific thing on the target page
- [ ] All [bracketed] placeholders replaced
- [ ] Subject line under 55 characters
- [ ] Send Tuesday-Thursday, 9-11 AM ET
- [ ] Logged in tracking-tracker.md
