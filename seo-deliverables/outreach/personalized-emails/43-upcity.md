# 43 - UpCity (B2B Services Directory)

- **Target site:** https://upcity.com
- **Contact:** Self-submission at upcity.com/get-listed
- **DR/DA:** 65
- **Topical fit:** B2B services directory; "Top X in Y" listings indexed well
- **Achievability:** Easy - free profile; 5-7 day review
- **Time-to-link:** 5-7 days
- **What we want:** Listed profile with dofollow link + category badges
- **Value to UpCity: Verified bootstrapped firm with detailed portfolio

---

## STEP 1 - Profile submission

URL: https://upcity.com/get-listed

Use canonical NAP block from `citation-submissions.md`. Fill ALL fields - UpCity ranks complete profiles higher in their directories.

Required:
- Company name, tagline, description
- HQ + service area
- Services (Custom Software Development, Cybersecurity, Penetration Testing, Web Development, Mobile App Development)
- Industries (E-commerce, Healthcare, Finance, Construction, Insurance, SaaS, Manufacturing)
- Pricing band ($125-200/hr; $5K min project)
- Year founded, team size
- 4-6 portfolio items with case study URLs
- Logo, hero image

---

## STEP 2 - Category badge applications

After listing is live, apply for relevant category badges:
- "Top Software Development Companies in Georgia"
- "Top Cybersecurity Companies in Atlanta"
- "Top Penetration Testing Firms"
- "Top Custom Software Development"

These badge pages are high-DA dofollow backlinks.

---

## STEP 3 - Client reviews

Same workflow as Clutch (rank 2) - ask 2-3 of the tier-1 clients to submit a UpCity review. UpCity's review process is faster than Clutch's (no phone call), so requesting these in the same week is fine.
