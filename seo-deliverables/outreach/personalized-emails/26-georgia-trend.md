**New Georgia C-Corp QUANT LAB USA scales custom software and cybersecurity hiring across Macon and Atlanta metro**

MACON, GA - QUANT LAB USA INC, a newly incorporated Georgia C-Corp (GA SOS #26086454, EIN 42-2039870, filed April 12, 2026), today announced active hiring across its Macon and Atlanta-metro service area. The firm is recruiting junior developers (TypeScript, Next.js, Postgres) and senior penetration testers (MITRE ATT&CK methodology) to support continued client demand.

Founded by William ("Bill") Beltz, QUANT LAB USA has shipped fourteen client engagements in the past twelve months. Clients span Atlanta-metro counties (Bridgepointe Painting across nine cities, Northcrest Fence & Gate in five), West Georgia (HobbsPeak), and out-of-state markets in fintech, healthcare, e-commerce, broadcast media, and trades verticals.

"Macon-headquartered, Atlanta-billing is the strategy, not a compromise," said Beltz. "Our customer is a 55-year-old fencing contractor in Marietta or a solo prop trader in Buckhead, not a Series A founder in San Francisco. The Macon base lets us run the firm sustainably while serving the Atlanta-metro book of business."

QUANT LAB USA pairs custom software development with optional MITRE ATT&CK-aligned penetration testing on every engagement. The firm has also productized internal tooling as J5 Sales OS, an AI-powered lead generation and CRM platform that landed its first paying customer cohort in early 2026.

**About QUANT LAB USA INC**

QUANT LAB USA is a custom software development and cybersecurity firm based in Douglasville, GA, serving clients across the Southeast US and nationwide remote.
