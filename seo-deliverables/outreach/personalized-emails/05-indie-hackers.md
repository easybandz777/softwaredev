## STEP 2 - First flagship post

**Topic (pick one for the first post; rotate the others for weeks 2-4):**

### Option A - Revenue retrospective

**Title:** I shipped 14 client builds in 12 months from Macon, GA. Here's what worked and what nearly killed me.

**Category:** Starting Up

**Hook:** A non-coastal, non-metro one-person dev shop's actual numbers from year one. No outside capital. No agency partners. Just trades and SMB customers.

**Body outline (target: 1,200-1,800 words):**
1. Where I started (Sept 2024): one client, $4K/month MRR-equivalent, contracting on the side
2. The niching decision (Dec 2024): pick blue-collar trades or pick AI SaaS founders; I picked trades because the customer is in the field and never reads Twitter
3. The first six builds (Q1 2025): Northcrest Fence, Bridgepointe Painting, HobbsPeak, ProtectWithBri, J5 Sales OS internal version, motorcycle shop
4. The second six (Q2-Q3): Wilder Recovery, Coastal Yacht, Northstar Trading Desk, Clear Channel Broadcast, Regional Medical Billing, contractor estimating engine
5. The "almost killed me" parts: a single client demanding scope creep at 80% completion; the J5 productization decision that ate 3 weeks I needed elsewhere; the cash-flow gap between client invoice cycles
6. The math: revenue, gross margin, hours per week, vacation days taken (zero)
7. What I would change: contracts, deposit policy, a 4-day workweek I am committing to in 2026
8. What I would keep: niche, geography, no outside capital

**Call to action at end:** "Anyone else running a solo dev shop with trades or service-business customers? Reply - I will respond to every comment. Working on a longer write-up at quantlabusa.dev/blog."

### Option B - Productization story

**Title:** I extracted an internal sales tool into a SaaS. It just hit its first paying cohort. Here's the agency-to-product transition that worked.

**Category:** Growth

**Body:** Same length, focus on J5 Sales OS - the agency cash flow funded the productization, why vertical SMB beats horizontal CRM at this tier, the first ten customers and the LinkedIn outbound motion.

### Option C - "Build in public for customers who don't use Twitter"

**Title:** My customers don't read tech Twitter. Here's how I do build-in-public anyway.

**Category:** Marketing

**Body:** Same length, focus on chamber of commerce, local newspaper press, Google Business Profile, word of mouth, SEO on city + service pages.
