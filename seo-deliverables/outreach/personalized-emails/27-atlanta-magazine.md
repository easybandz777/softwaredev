Hi Allison,

Your Atlanta Magazine profile of the Athens music-tech intersection last fall was the kind of long-form that does what only ATL Mag can do well - finding the cultural angle in business stories. I want to pitch a complementary piece on Macon.

I am Bill Beltz, founder of QUANT LAB USA - a custom software and cybersecurity firm based in Macon. The story I want to pitch is not really about my company; it is about the pattern. Macon-Bibb has:

- A Telegraph that is now digital-only
- A Mercer engineering school producing graduates who do not want to move to Midtown
- A downtown that is gentrifying around music venues (Capricorn Sound Studios reopened)
- A small but real cluster of independent software shops billing Atlanta-metro clients while paying Macon rents

The cultural arbitrage is the story.

What I can offer Atlanta Magazine:

- A first-person essay (1,800-2,500 words) on running a software firm from Macon while servicing Atlanta-metro clients - the cultural arbitrage angle, not the business case
- Access to three other Macon and Middle Georgia founders who would speak on record about the same dynamic
- Photo subjects: the Douglasville office, the Macon coffee shops that double as remote-work hubs, the drive between, the Mercer campus
- Day-in-the-life threading - leaving the house in Macon at 7 AM, video call with a Buckhead client at 10, drive to Alpharetta for a site visit at 2, back in Macon for dinner

The piece pairs naturally with ATL Mag's existing "Atlanta is a region, not a city" coverage.

This is a 6-12 week pitch given Atlanta Magazine's lead time. Happy to scope tighter (essay only) or wider (profile + sidebar interviews).
