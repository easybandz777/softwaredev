# Outreach Tracking Tracker

**Owner:** Bill Beltz - beltz@quantlabusa.dev - (770) 652-1282
**Updated:** [Bill updates every Friday afternoon]

This is the day-to-day working tracker for the PRIORITY-50 campaign. Update within 60 minutes of every send, every reply, every follow-up. The 00-MASTER-TRACKER.md (one directory up) is the rolled-up summary - this file is the granular working log.

**Status legend:**
- `not-sent` - Not yet emailed
- `sent` - Initial email out, awaiting response
- `f1-sent` - First follow-up sent
- `f2-sent` - Second follow-up sent
- `replied` - Got a reply (any kind)
- `accepted` - Editor/host accepted the pitch
- `landed` - Link is LIVE
- `declined` - Explicit no
- `paused` - Awaiting prerequisite (e.g., OSS repo not yet live)
- `stop` - Killed; do not pursue further

## Caveats and rules

- Maximum 5 cold-pitch sends per calendar day to avoid Gmail rate flags
- Maximum 5 client-review requests per calendar week (per review-requests.md)
- Tuesday-Thursday 9-11 AM ET is the optimal send window
- After landing a link, update the row's "Link earned (URL)" column AND the cumulative summary
- After a "declined" or "stop" status, do not pursue for 90 days minimum
- If a pitch lands an article, share the article on LinkedIn / Twitter within 24 hours and email the editor a thank-you with one piece of substantive feedback
