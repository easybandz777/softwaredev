# Anti-pattern checklist (DO NOT do these)

- [ ] Do NOT send PR pitches on Friday after 12 PM ET or Monday before 9 AM ET
- [ ] Do NOT send LinkedIn DMs on Friday after 3 PM ET or Monday before 9 AM ET
- [ ] Do NOT cluster review requests (max 5/week, spaced 2-3 days)
- [ ] Do NOT review-gate ("would you give us 5 stars?" before sending review link)
- [ ] Do NOT offer compensation for reviews
- [ ] Do NOT use AI-generated copy on outlets that have explicit no-AI policies (The New Stack, Dark Reading, The Register, Hacker News)
- [ ] Do NOT pitch the same outlet twice in 30 days without a fresh angle
- [ ] Do NOT skip pre-send personalization checks - reference 1 recent piece per pitch
- [ ] Do NOT send F1 immediately on accept - wait 24-48 hours minimum
- [ ] Do NOT use automation tools that violate LinkedIn TOS (Dux-Soup, Phantombuster) - manual sends only
