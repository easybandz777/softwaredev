## How to Use This Playbook

This is a 6-track outbound system. Each track targets one Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) and runs a 4-touch sequence over ~21 days. The math is straightforward: at a 25-35% connection-accept rate and a 6-12% qualified-reply rate from accepted connections, sending 25 well-researched requests per ICP per week generates roughly 1-3 qualified conversations weekly per track. Six tracks in parallel = 6-18 qualified conversations per week at full volume.

### Tooling

- **LinkedIn Sales Navigator (Advanced plan, $99/mo)** is mandatory. The free LinkedIn search throttles at ~30 commercial searches/month and lacks the filters this playbook depends on (years in current role, seniority + function combined, company headcount growth, recent job change).
- **A CRM or tracking sheet.** Use J5 Sales OS or a simple Airtable. Track: profile URL, ICP track, connection sent date, accept date, F1/F2/F3 send dates, reply status, reply quality (1-5), booked status.
- **A scheduler** for sending. Don't use auto-tools that violate LinkedIn TOS (Dux-Soup, Phantombuster) — they get accounts restricted. Send manually in 30-minute blocks, twice daily. Realistic ceiling: 80-120 personalized connection requests per week per account.
- **A founder-voice tone library.** Read every message out loud before sending. If it sounds like a template, rewrite it.

### Daily / Weekly Cadence

- **Monday-Friday, 9-10 AM ET:** Send 10-15 new connection requests across rotating ICP tracks.
- **Monday-Friday, 4-5 PM ET:** Send follow-ups to accepted connections. F1 goes 1-2 business days after accept. F2 goes 5-7 days after F1. Final goes 14 days after F2.
- **Friday afternoon:** Audit the week. Pull metrics. Kill the worst-performing variant.

### Response-Rate Benchmarks (industry, refined for founder-led B2B services in 2025-2026)

| Metric | Floor | Target | Excellent |
|---|---|---|---|
| Connection accept rate | 22% | 32% | 45%+ |
| Reply rate (F1) on accepts | 8% | 14% | 22%+ |
| Reply rate (F2) on F1 non-responders | 4% | 7% | 12%+ |
| Reply rate (final) on F2 non-responders | 2% | 4% | 7%+ |
| **Qualified reply rate, full sequence** | 6% | 12% | 20%+ |
| Booked-call rate from qualified replies | 25% | 40% | 60%+ |

If any track underperforms the floor for two consecutive weeks, kill it or rewrite the opener.

### What Counts as a Qualified Reply

A reply only counts as "qualified" if the prospect (a) answers the implicit question rather than deflecting, (b) is the actual decision-maker or one degree removed, and (c) opens a door — "tell me more," "send a link," "let's talk next week," or "we're already working with X but..." Anything else — auto-reply, "not interested," "I left that company" — counts as a closed loop, not a qualified reply.

### A/B Variant Discipline

Each connection request below has two variants:

- **Variant A — Specific Compliment.** Lead with something you saw on their profile or company news. Pure rapport, no ask.
- **Variant B — Specific Question.** Lead with a pointed question about their stack, role, or workflow. Higher signal, lower accept rate.

Run a 50/50 split for the first 100 requests per ICP. After 100, kill the loser. Don't run more than two variants at once or you lose statistical power.

### Profile-Research Floor

Every connection request must reference at least one specific, verifiable detail from the prospect's profile. If you cannot fill in the bracketed personalization variables honestly in under 90 seconds of profile review, skip the prospect. Templated outreach without personalization performs at <5% accept and trashes account reputation.

## Appendix A — Sequence Hygiene Rules

1. **Never send F1, F2, or Final on a Friday after 3 PM ET or Monday before 9 AM ET.** Weekend buffer kills urgency. Tuesday-Thursday morning sends outperform by 1.4-1.8x in reply rate.
2. **If a prospect replies at any stage, stop the sequence immediately.** Even if the reply is "not interested," it counts as a complete loop. Move them to a 90-day quiet list and reconsider.
3. **Never paste the same F1 to two prospects in the same 24-hour window without changing at least one personalization variable.** LinkedIn fingerprints duplicate-content messages and throttles accounts that template at volume.
4. **If your accept rate drops below 18% for a week, pause sending for 72 hours.** Account reputation recovers; bad reputation compounds. Use the pause to rewrite openers.
5. **Track replies by sentiment, not just count.** A "send a link" reply that converts to a meeting is worth 20 "thanks but no" replies. Optimize for the former.

## Appendix B — When to Escalate to a Direct Email or Call

If a prospect:
- Connects and engages with your posts but doesn't reply to F1/F2,
- Or replies positively to F1 but goes dark on F2,

… find their work email (Hunter.io, Apollo, RocketReach) and send a 60-word email with subject line `"Following up — [Company]"`. Reference the LinkedIn thread. Do not send more than one email. Move on after.

This is the only place email is allowed in this playbook — everything else lives on LinkedIn.
