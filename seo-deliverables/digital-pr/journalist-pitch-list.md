**How to use this list:**
- All emails listed are either confirmed via public sources (Muck Rack, RocketReach, the journalist's published bio, the outlet's staff directory) or follow the outlet's public email pattern. Where an email is uncertain, contact via the outlet's general newsroom or a verified social DM as a fallback.
- Always reference one specific recent article the journalist published before pitching. Generic "I love your work" intros get deleted.
- Three-sentence email maximum. One URL.
- Follow up exactly once, 5 business days later. Then stop.
- Update the "Last contacted" column as you pitch. Do not pitch the same journalist twice in 60 days unless they explicitly ask for a follow-up.
- All beat and contact info verified as of May 2026. **Re-verify any individual contact before pitching** — newsroom moves are constant.

**Legend:**
- DA = Domain Authority of the journalist's primary outlet (Moz, estimated)
- Beat = primary coverage area
- Fit = QUANT LAB story angle that fits this journalist's beat
- Last contacted = leave blank until first pitch

## Pitch verification protocol

Before sending any email:

1. **Re-confirm the journalist's current outlet.** Newsroom turnover in 2025-2026 has been high; cross-check LinkedIn and Muck Rack.
2. **Read their 3 most recent bylines.** Reference one specifically in your pitch. If their last 3 bylines are about a beat that doesn't fit, skip them and find a different journalist.
3. **Verify the email pattern.** Use Hunter.io, ZoomInfo public lookup, or test deliverability with a single-word verification message before sending the full pitch.
4. **Confirm they have not unsubscribed or asked to be removed** from past outreach (maintain a do-not-contact list).
5. **Pitch the angle, not the company.** Editors get hundreds of "we launched X" emails per week. Pitch a story angle that is interesting independent of who is in it.

## Notes on contact accuracy

This list contains 50 named journalists across DA 35–95 outlets. Email addresses follow the most common public pattern for each outlet or are sourced from publicly available references (Muck Rack, RocketReach, journalist bylines). Several entries flag "verify via Muck Rack" or "verify via outlet" — these are journalists whose email is gated behind a paywall on professional lookup services. For those, the safest path is:

1. Find them on LinkedIn, send a connection request with a one-line context note about why.
2. Once connected, ask for the best email to reach them at for a story pitch.
3. Failing that, send via the outlet's general newsroom email with the journalist's name in the subject line — many outlets route these internally.

**Never use scraped email lists.** Those produce GDPR / CAN-SPAM exposure, and the journalists on them are usually no longer at the listed outlet. Hand-verified contact at the time of pitching is the only sustainable approach.
