## Section 6 — Negative Review Recovery (1- or 2-star)

### 6a. Public reply (≤200 characters, founder voice, professional, names the issue, invites offline)

**Template:**

```
[First Name], this is William, the owner. I hear you on [specific issue — "the timeline slip" / "the missed call" / "the bug after launch"]. Please call me directly at (770) 652-1282 so I can make it right.
```

**Rules for the public reply:**
- Always sign as William, the owner — not "the team."
- Name the actual issue. Generic "sorry you had a bad experience" replies look defensive and make every other reader trust you less.
- Never argue facts in public. Never blame the client. Never blame a contractor.
- Always give a direct phone number, not just an email — it signals you're serious.
- Reply within 24 hours. Same business day if possible.

### 6b. Private follow-up email (sent within 24 hours of the review)

**Subject:** Owner-to-owner — let's make this right

## Section 7 — Reviewer-Targeting Playbook

### Prioritization criteria (in order)

1. **Sentiment proxy** — Did the client express enthusiasm in writing (text, email, Slack)? Did they refer someone unprompted? Did they extend or expand the contract? This is the #1 signal.
2. **Project size & visibility** — Larger, more public deployments produce richer review content. Real product launches > small one-off site fixes.
3. **Communication frequency** — Clients who responded fast and stayed engaged are far likelier to follow through on a review request.
4. **Time since project end** — Sweet spot is **2–6 weeks after launch / handoff**. Earlier than 2 weeks the work isn't yet "real" to them; later than 6 weeks the momentum is gone.
5. **No prior ask** — Never re-ask a client who already reviewed (or already declined) for the same project.

## Section 9 — Cadence + Cap Rules

**Hard rules — do not violate.**

1. **One ask per client per project.** Same client, new project = new ask is allowed. Same client, same project = never re-ask after a "no" or non-response past the follow-up cap.
2. **Maximum two follow-ups per ask.**
   - Follow-up 1: +7 days after initial ask (Section 3 template)
   - Follow-up 2: +14 days after initial ask, only if it feels natural and the relationship supports it. If unsure, skip it.
   - After follow-up 2, stop. Move the row to `declined` if no response.
3. **Cooling-off period after a "no":** 12 months minimum before any review-related outreach to that client again, and only on a new project.
4. **Never offer compensation for a review** — no discounts, no free hours, no gift cards. Violates Google policy.
5. **Never review-gate.** Don't pre-screen clients with "would you give us 5 stars?" before sending the link. Send the link regardless of expected sentiment and let the offline-reply line catch unhappy clients.
6. **Negative review SLA:** Public reply within 24 hours. Private email within 24 hours. Resolution conversation initiated within 72 hours.
7. **Update-ask SLA:** Only send the Section 6c update-ask after the client explicitly confirms the issue is resolved AND ≥7 days have passed since resolution to confirm it stuck.
8. **Volume cap:** No more than 5 review requests sent in a single calendar week. Spaced asks beat batch asks — Google's spam detection flags clustered review activity.
9. **Channel cap:** Pick ONE channel per ask. Do not send the email and the SMS the same day. If using email-then-SMS as the follow-up, the SMS counts as follow-up 1.
10. **Document everything in the tracking sheet** — every row, every send, every response. The sheet is the source of truth.

## Appendix — Voice & Tone Guidelines

- **Founder voice, always.** Sign every template as William, not "the team."
- **Short over long.** A 90-word email beats a 200-word email every time.
- **Specific over generic.** Never send "thanks for working with us" — always reference the actual project and the actual outcome.
- **Offline escape hatch always present.** Every ask includes a "reply here if anything went sideways" line.
- **No emoji.** Inconsistent with the brand voice on the rest of the site.
- **No "5 stars" language.** Don't ask for a specific rating. Just ask for a review.
