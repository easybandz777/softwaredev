---

## What makes a keyword a "quick win" here

All 20 satisfy every one of these tests:

1. **Low keyword difficulty (KD 1-2).** The top 5 organic results are weak: directory pages, thin blog posts, or off-topic generalists. We can outrank with a real subject-matter post.
2. **Decent commercial intent.** Not pure info; the searcher is researching for an eventual purchase decision (commercial) or already comparing vendors (transactional).
3. **Single-page satisfiable.** One great 1,500-2,500 word post + matching schema + good internal links is enough. No pillar architecture required.
4. **Slots into an existing pillar.** Inherits topical authority from already-ranking content — that's what compresses the timeline from 6 months to 30-60 days.
5. **No bare-brand "quantlab" play.** Per the brand-search competitor reality.
6. **Realistic 30-60 day ranking horizon assumes:** the page ships in week 1, gets at least 1 internal link from a higher-authority page on day 1, and is submitted to IndexNow / GSC on day 1.

**Volume buckets are gut estimates, not tool-validated** — same caveat as `long-tail-100.md`.

## Tracking these in GSC and analytics

For each quick-win page, set up:

1. **Google Search Console:** Manually request indexing on day 1; monitor average position weekly for 60 days
2. **GA4 custom event:** Use existing `social_click` and `lead_magnet_download` events; add UTM-tagged internal links from referring posts
3. **Conversion tracking:** Tag every CTA with `service_interest` matching the page's pillar
4. **Weekly review:** Sunday 15-min check: which of the 20 are gaining impressions but not clicks? (Title/meta rewrite signal.) Which are getting clicks but not converting? (CTA copy or page-bottom layout issue.)

**Kill criteria (after 60 days):**
- Page below position 30 → rewrite title + add 2 more internal links from higher-authority pages
- Page at position 11-30 with no clicks → rewrite meta description
- Page at position 1-10 with no leads → rewrite CTA + lead magnet pairing
- Page at position 1-10 generating leads → expand into a pillar (add 2 sibling spokes pointing in)
