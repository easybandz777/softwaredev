# QUANT LAB USA — Topical Cluster Map (Wave 6)

**Date:** 2026-05-16
**Companion to:** `long-tail-100.md`
**Purpose:** Show how every Wave 6 keyword opportunity slots into a topical authority pillar and connects (via internal links) to the existing 140+ live pages.

This is the architectural blueprint: which pillar each new page reinforces, and the exact hub-and-spoke links that pass equity in both directions.

---

## How to read this document

Each pillar has:
1. **Hub page** — the topical authority root. Almost always an existing service / industry / blog pillar.
2. **Existing spokes** — pages already live that link up to the hub.
3. **New spokes (Wave 6)** — pages from `long-tail-100.md` to add, with their long-tail row numbers.
4. **Cross-pillar bridges** — pages that connect this pillar to another, sealing the topical graph.

Internal-link rule: every new spoke must link **up** to its pillar AND **across** to at least one cross-pillar bridge. Every pillar must link **down** to its top 5 spokes from the hub copy.
