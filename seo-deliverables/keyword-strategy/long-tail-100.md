## Methodology and disclaimers

**Volume estimates are gut estimates, not tool-validated.** We don't have Ahrefs/SEMrush access in this worktree. Buckets are tuned against May-2026 SERP saturation observed in WebSearch results (number of ranking competitors, recency, content depth):

- **Low** ≈ <300 US monthly searches. Few competing pages, often deeply niche or geo-modified.
- **Med** ≈ 300–2,500 US monthly searches. Multiple competing pages of similar depth, recent freshness.
- **High** ≈ 2,500+ US monthly searches. Dozens of pages, news amplification, broad commercial intent.

**Keyword Difficulty (KD) 1–5** is also estimated, based on:
- Domain authority of the top 5 organic results
- How many directory-style pages dominate (Clutch / DesignRush / GoodFirms = signal of competitive saturation)
- Whether incumbents (Quantlab Financial, big agencies) own the SERP
- How geo-modified the query is (more modifiers → lower KD for us)

**Intent codes:** **I**nformational, **C**ommercial (research / evaluation), **T**ransactional (ready to buy/contact)

**Priority tiers:**
- **Tier 1 (must-do, next 30 days):** Strong commercial intent + we can credibly win in 60–90 days + slot-in to an existing pillar. 25 keywords.
- **Tier 2 (do in 30–90 days):** Solid intent but requires net-new pillar or builds on Tier-1 momentum. 40 keywords.
- **Tier 3 (do in 90+ days or kill if signal is weak):** Either highly competitive head terms we'll need authority for, or speculative niches worth a single test page. 35 keywords.

**Brand reality enforced:** No bare-brand "quantlab" plays (owned by Quantlab Financial, est. 1998). Every query is USA-qualified, geo-modified, vertical-specific, or solution-aware.

**Duplicate scrub:** Cross-referenced against `seo-deliverables/02-content-gap-keywords.md` and `08-blog-topics.md`. Where a recommended target overlaps an existing post, the row notes "REFRESH" or "EXPAND" rather than "NEW."

## What was deliberately excluded

- **Bare "quantlab" brand-search variants.** Owned by Quantlab Financial (est. 1998). See `project_brand_search_competitor.md` memory.
- **"Best software developers" head terms without geo/industry qualifier.** SERP fully owned by Clutch / DesignRush / G2 — burning content budget on these for years would yield nothing.
- **Generic "AI development" head terms.** Saturated by every dev shop on earth. We only target AI + qualifier (AI agent for support, AI integration cost, AI automation consultant + city).
- **"Hire developer" without stack or geo.** Captured by Upwork / Toptal / LinkedIn. We only target "hire [stack] developer [usa|city]".
- **Pure tech-jargon glossary expansions.** Glossary already covers 39 terms; further additions are diminishing returns vs net-new conversion pages.
